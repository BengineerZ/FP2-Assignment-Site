Title: Massachusetts Municipal Boundaries
Table: mapc_municipalities
Type: geojson

Data Source:
Massachusetts Municipal Boundaries:
    Source: MassGIS
    Referring URL: https://datacommon.mapc.org/browser/datasets/390
    Last Updated: March 2017

Description:
This dataset provides polygon representations of municipal boundaries for the 351 communities in the Commonwealth of Massachusetts. It was developed to maintain an up-to-date version of town boundaries, with specific focus on the 101 cities and towns in Metro Boston covered by MAPC. Some boundaries could not be definitively mapped due to legislative ambiguities, and these are noted within the attribute information.

Attributes:
    objectid:
        Type: Integer
        Description: Internal feature number.
        Example: 4

    muni_id:
        Type: Integer
        Description: Unique municipal identifier.
        Example: 92

    municipal:
        Type: String
        Description: The name of the municipality.
        Example: Essex

    geometry:
        Type: GeoJSON MultiPolygon
        Description: The geographic representation of the municipal boundary.
        Example: MULTIPOLYGON (((259231.582 936048.315, 259275.092 936028.815, 259311.592 936048.315)))
